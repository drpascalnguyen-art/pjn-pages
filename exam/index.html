Your first visit

What's actually in a new patient exam with me

Many patients tell me they've felt rushed and that nobody looked at the whole picture. My exam is different. I'm not in a hurry, and I want to hear your story first. Here's everything we look at, and why.

Everything we look at

1. We listen first

Your health history, your symptoms, what you've already tried and your goals. You won't be rushed.

2. 3D (CBCT) scan

A 3D view of your teeth, jaws, sinuses and airway. It shows infection, bone loss and anatomy that flat x-rays can miss.

3. Digital x-rays and photos

A detailed record of your teeth so we can track changes over time.

4. Oral cancer screening

A careful check of your tongue, cheeks, throat and lymph nodes.

5. Gum health

We measure your gums and check for bleeding and inflammation, which is linked to the rest of your health.

6. Decay and wear

We look for cavities, cracks and worn enamel, and what's causing them.

7. Bite and jaw joints (TMJ)

We check how your teeth meet, your jaw joints, your range of motion and tender muscles.

8. Digital scan of your bite

A 3D scan that shows wear patterns and stress on your teeth from grinding or clenching.

9. Airway and posture

We look for signs of mouth breathing, snoring and sleep-disordered breathing, and how head and neck posture relate to your airway.

10. Saliva and pH

Saliva protects your teeth. We check its flow and acidity, which affect decay and enamel erosion.

11. Blood sugar (A1C)

Blood sugar affects inflammation, gum health and healing.

12. Nitric oxide

Gives insight into circulation, which matters for healing.

13. Heart rate variability (HRV)

A look at nervous system balance and stress resilience.

14. Minerals

We talk about the minerals your teeth, gums and bone rely on.

15. Meridian assessment

A traditional tooth-organ chart we use as one extra perspective alongside the clinical exam.

What you leave with

  • A clear explanation of what we found, in plain language
  • Your options, including doing nothing, with the pros and cons of each
  • A written plan and estimate before any treatment starts

Not every test is right for every patient. We'll tell you which ones make sense for you.

— Dr. Pascal Nguyen, DMD, NMD
