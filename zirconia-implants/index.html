Zirconia implants: metal-free, and honest about who they fit

Zirconia is a white ceramic implant for people who want to replace a tooth without metal. It's a great option for many patients, and not the right one for everyone. Here's how we decide.

Why patients ask for zirconia

  • Metal-free. A good fit if you prefer to avoid metal in your body, or have a known or suspected metal sensitivity.
  • Naturally white. No grey shadow showing through thin gums.
  • Doesn't corrode. Zirconia is a ceramic, so it doesn't rust or corrode the way metals can.
  • Gum-friendly surface. Studies suggest plaque may collect less on zirconia than on titanium. Your home care still matters most.

Questions we hear every day

Don't zirconia implants break?

Early generations had more fracture problems. Today's designs are much stronger, and research on current implants for single teeth shows results comparable to titanium. Fractures are still possible, which is why careful case selection matters: we look at your bite, any grinding or clenching, and how much bone you have before recommending one.

How much does a zirconia implant cost?

It depends on your case: one tooth or several, whether you need bone grafting or an extraction first, and the type of crown. We give you an exact, written estimate at your consultation, before any treatment starts.

Am I a good candidate?

Often yes if you're replacing one or a few teeth, have enough healthy bone, and don't have heavy grinding. We confirm with a 3D (CBCT) scan, a bite analysis and a full exam.

What happens at the first visit?

We start by listening: your health history, your goals and any concerns about materials. Then we take a 3D scan and check your bone, gums and bite. You leave knowing your options, including whether zirconia makes sense for you.

Can you test which materials suit me?

Yes. We offer biocompatibility testing for patients who want extra information before choosing dental materials.
